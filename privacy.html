Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 2, 2026

Kaden's Racing Championships is a racing game that can be played solo or with online global play features.

Information We Collect

When online play is enabled, the game may save your gamer nickname, player ID, selected car, race score, lap time, track, race position, play activity, and temporary online presence so other players can see active racers.

Do not use your real name as your gamer nickname.

How We Use Information

We use this information to show leaderboards, online player counts, multiplayer presence, race results, and saved game preferences.

Solo Mode

Solo mode keeps gameplay private on your device and does not intentionally publish online presence or leaderboard records.

Storage

Some settings and progress are stored locally in your browser or app web view. Online play data is stored in the game database.

Delete Account

You can delete your KRC account data from the Settings page. This removes local KRC data from the device and requests deletion of server records tied to your player ID.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center on iOS, Apple may process leaderboard scores according to Apple's privacy policy. Leaderboard participation is optional.

Children and Safety

Players should use a nickname and avoid sharing personal information. Public display names may be visible to other players when online features are enabled.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact the app owner through the support contact listed on the App Store product page.
