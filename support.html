Quick fixes

  • Online offline: Switch to Solo on the main menu, or turn Global Online back on in Settings when you have a connection.
  • Graphics / frame rate: Settings → Graphics → Medium or Low.
  • Controls: Settings → Driving — choose wheel, tilt, or D-pad.
  • Progress reset: Settings → Data (this cannot be undone).
