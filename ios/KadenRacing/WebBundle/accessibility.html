Accessibility

Kaden's Racing Championships · iPhone and iPad

Kaden's Racing Championships is a dark-themed racing game. Menus, Settings, garage, pause, and results follow system accessibility settings. Racing still uses touch, tilt, or a wheel.

What the App Store labels cover

We indicate support for Dark Interface, Larger Text, Reduced Motion, Sufficient Contrast, and Differentiate Without Color Alone — you can finish menu, garage, Settings, and race-setup tasks with those features.

We do not yet claim VoiceOver or Voice Control for a full race. Driving needs analog steer and throttle. VoiceOver can still read menus, pause, HUD summary, and race callouts.

Built-in support

  • Dark Interface: Menus stay dark. Turn on Darker races in Settings → Accessibility, or use Night race, for darker track lighting.
  • Larger Text: Menu and Settings type scales with iOS text size (including accessibility sizes).
  • Reduced Motion: Menu parallax, flame breathe, grid intro camera, and finish bursts stop when Reduce Motion is on.
  • Increase Contrast: Stronger text, HUD panels, and numeric nitro/heat values.
  • Differentiate without color: Position is spoken and labeled (P 1 / 8). Meters have text names, not color alone.
  • VoiceOver: Labels on Play, Settings, pause, pedals, D-pad, and a spoken HUD. Race callouts speak 3-2-1-Go, position, and wrong way.

In-app settings

Open Settings → Accessibility:

  • Race callouts
  • Darker races
  • Larger race controls

VoiceOver also increases steering assist so the car stays on the road more easily.

Known limits

  • You cannot complete a race using only VoiceOver or Voice Control. Gas, brake, and steer still need touch, tilt, a wheel, or a game controller.
  • The 3D track is not a VoiceOver rotor. Use pause and callouts for status.
